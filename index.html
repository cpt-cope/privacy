Privacy Policy

Cope and Son Entertainment / Money Night Live
App: TDIM Publisher
Effective date: October 7, 2026

What this app is

TDIM Publisher is an internal tool used only by Cope and Son Entertainment to manage our own Facebook Page and our own ad account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else logs in to it.

What data the app uses

  • Statistics for our own Facebook Page and our own ads, such as reach, views, clicks and spend.
  • Posts that we publish to our own Page.

The app does not collect, store or process personal data about people who view, like, comment on or interact with our Page or ads. Any statistics we read are the totals Meta provides, not information about individual people.

How we use it

We use these statistics only to understand how our own videos and ads are doing, and to publish our own content. We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.

Data kept and deleted

The app does not keep a database of user information. Because we collect no personal data, there is no user data to delete. If you believe we hold any data about you, contact us (below) and we will check and delete it.

Meta's own handling of data

Facebook and Instagram are run by Meta, which handles data under its own policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions or data requests: send a message to the Money Night Live Facebook Page.
